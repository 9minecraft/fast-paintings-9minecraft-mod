# Fast Paintings — Place Wall Art Exactly Where You Want It

Fast Paintings 9minecraft Mod turns regular Minecraft paintings into solid wall blocks that snap flat against surfaces. It gives you full control over your artwork placement without the random sizes or loose entity behavior of vanilla paintings. You can place single painting blocks one by one, and placing them side by side combines them into larger custom pictures. The mod keeps all classic vanilla art styles while making wall decorating fast and reliable.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **MehVahdJukaar**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
